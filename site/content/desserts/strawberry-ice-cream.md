# Strawberry Ice Cream

Custard-based strawberry ice cream with macerated berries for deep colour and flavour. Makes about 1 litre.

## Ingredients

### Strawberries

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh strawberries, hulled | 530g |
| Caster sugar | 65g |
| Lemon juice | 2 tsp |

### Custard

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Double cream | 265ml |
| Whole milk | 130ml |
| Egg yolks | 3 |
| Caster sugar | 65g |
| Salt | pinch |

## Method

### 1. Macerate the strawberries

Roughly chop the strawberries, toss with 65g sugar and the lemon juice. Leave for 30–60 minutes until they release their juices. Blend about two-thirds until smooth, leave the rest chunky. Set aside.

### 2. Make the custard base

Whisk 3 egg yolks with 65g caster sugar and a pinch of salt until pale.

Heat the cream and milk in a saucepan until just steaming — not boiling. Pour the hot cream slowly into the egg yolks, whisking constantly. Return everything to the pan over medium-low heat and cook, stirring with a spatula, until the custard coats the back of a spoon and reads **82–84°C** on a thermometer. Don't let it boil.

### 3. Combine and chill

Strain the custard through a fine sieve into a bowl. Stir in the blended strawberry mixture. Press cling film directly onto the surface and refrigerate for **at least 4 hours**, ideally overnight.

### 4. Churn

Pour into your ice cream maker and churn according to the machine's instructions. Fold in the reserved chunky strawberry pieces in the last few minutes of churning.

Transfer to a freezer-safe container, press cling film onto the surface, and freeze for **at least 2 hours** to firm up.

### 5. Serve

Remove from the freezer 10–15 minutes before serving to temper.

## Notes

- **Maceration is key.** The sugar draws out the strawberry juices and intensifies the flavour — skipping this step gives you a washed-out, icy result.
- **Sweetness:** taste the chilled base before churning. It should taste slightly too sweet — freezing dulls sweetness significantly.
- **No ice cream maker?** Pour into a shallow container and freeze. Stir vigorously with a fork every 45 minutes for about 4 hours until set.
- **Colour:** the maceration gives a deep natural colour without cooking the strawberries, which would dull their fresh flavour.
- **Egg whites:** save them for meringues or freeze for later.
- **Keeps:** 2 weeks in the freezer before ice crystals start to develop.
