# Apple Crumble

Classic British apple crumble — soft, sharp-sweet apples under a thick, buttery, crunchy oat topping. Serves 6.

## Apple filling

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apples (half cooking, half eating — see notes) | 1kg |
| Butter | 15g |
| Light brown sugar | 50g |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp |
| Ground cinnamon | 1 tsp |
| Plain flour | 1 tbsp |

## Crumble topping

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plain flour | 200g |
| Cold butter, cubed | 120g |
| Demerara sugar | 100g |
| Rolled oats | 50g |
| Pinch of salt | |

## Method

### 1. Make the topping

Rub the cold butter into the flour and salt with your fingertips until it looks like coarse breadcrumbs, with a few pea-sized lumps of butter left. Stir in the demerara sugar and oats. Squeeze a few handfuls together to make clumps — these become the crunchy nuggets.

Put it in the fridge while you do the apples. Cold topping = crunchier crumble.

### 2. Prepare the apples

Heat the oven to **180°C fan**.

Peel, core, and cut the apples into ~2cm chunks. Toss with the lemon juice as you go.

Melt 15g butter in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the apples, brown sugar, and cinnamon and cook for **5 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the apples just start to soften and release some juice. The cooking apples will start breaking down; the eating apples should hold their shape. Stir in 1 tbsp flour to thicken the juices.

### 3. Assemble

Tip the apples into a ~2 litre baking dish (roughly 20×30cm) and spread level. Scatter the crumble topping evenly over the top — don't press it down. Keep it loose and lumpy.

### 4. Bake

Bake for **35–40 minutes** until the topping is deep golden and the fruit is bubbling up around the edges. If the top colours too fast, cover loosely with foil for the last 10 minutes.

### 5. Serve

Rest for 10 minutes — the filling is volcanic straight out of the oven and thickens as it cools. Serve warm with the salted caramel ice cream, custard, or cream.

## Notes

- **Apples:** a mix gives the best texture — cooking apples (Bramley) collapse into a sharp purée, eating apples (Braeburn, Cox, Granny Smith) hold their shape. All eating apples works too; reduce the cooking time to 3 minutes. **In Poland:** Szara Reneta or Antonówka are perfect cooking apples; pair with Ligol, Champion, or Golden.
- **Precooking the apples** stops the dreaded gap between a sunken filling and a domed topping, and means the fruit is properly soft by the time the crumble is crisp.
- **Make ahead:** the raw topping keeps 3 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer — bake straight from frozen, adding 5–10 minutes. You can assemble the whole thing a few hours ahead and keep it in the fridge.
- **Variations:** swap 50g of the flour for ground almonds, add 50g flaked almonds or chopped hazelnuts to the topping, or add a handful of blackberries to the apples.
- **Sugar in Poland:** demerara is sold as **cukier trzcinowy** (brown cane sugar, the coarse crystal kind). For the filling, any brown sugar works — or regular cukier biały if that's what you have.
- **Keeps:** 3 days in the fridge. Reheat at 160°C fan for 15 minutes to re-crisp the top.
