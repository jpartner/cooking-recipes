# Chocolate Fondant

Individual molten chocolate cakes — a set, cake-like edge and a liquid chocolate centre. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | In Poland | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, plus extra for the moulds | Masło ekstra 82% | 120g |
| Dark chocolate (70%), finely chopped | Czekolada gorzka 70% (Wedel, Wawel, or Lindt) | 120g |
| Eggs | Jajka | 2 |
| Egg yolks | Żółtka | 2 |
| Caster sugar | Cukier drobny, or zwykły cukier biały | 60g |
| Plain flour | Mąka pszenna tortowa typ 450, or typ 500 | 40g |
| Fine salt | Sól | a pinch |
| Cocoa powder, for dusting | Kakao (niesłodzone) — not kakao rozpuszczalne | |

## Method

### 1. Prepare the moulds

Heat the oven to **200°C fan**. Put a baking tray in to heat up — the fondants bake on it, which helps the bases set.

Butter four 150ml ramekins generously, right up the sides. Dust with cocoa, tipping out the excess. Cocoa releases more reliably than flour and does not leave a pale film.

### 2. Melt the chocolate

Melt the butter and chocolate together in a heatproof bowl set over a pan of barely simmering water. The bowl should not touch the water. Stir until smooth. Short bursts in the microwave work too. Set aside for a few minutes — hot chocolate will scramble the eggs.

### 3. Mix the batter

Whisk the eggs, yolks, and sugar until slightly thickened and a little paler — about a minute by hand. You do not need a full ribbon.

Stir in the melted chocolate, then fold in the flour and salt until just combined. The batter should be glossy and pourable, with no streaks of flour.

### 4. Fill

Divide evenly between the ramekins. They should be about two-thirds full. Tap once on the counter to level the tops.

Bake them now, or cover and refrigerate for up to 24 hours (see notes).

### 5. Bake

Bake on the hot tray for **8–9 minutes**. The edges should be set and just starting to come away from the ramekin. The centre should still wobble when you nudge the dish. A skewer pushed in about 1cm from the edge comes out clean. Pushed into the middle, it comes out with wet batter.

If the ramekins were in the fridge, add **1–2 minutes**.

### 6. Serve

Rest for 1 minute. Run a small knife around the edge, put a plate on top, and flip. Lift the ramekin off. Serve immediately — the centre keeps cooking on the plate. Chocolate ice cream, salted caramel ice cream, or cold cream all work.

## Notes

- **The window is short.** A minute too long and you have a small chocolate cake with no molten centre. The first time, bake one as a test and cut it open so you learn what your oven does. Leave the other three covered at room temperature until you are ready.
- **Don't open the oven** in the first 7 minutes. A drop in heat collapses the fondants.
- **Chocolate:** 64–70% melts into a sauce in the middle. Above 75% the centre sets firmer and can taste bitter — use 100g chocolate and 70g sugar.
- **Caster sugar** is white sugar with finer crystals than ordinary granulated, so it dissolves into the eggs. In Poland that is **cukier drobny**, sometimes labelled "do wypieków". Diamant and Südzucker both sell it. Regular **cukier biały** works — whisk for an extra minute. Do not use **cukier puder**.
- **Cocoa for the ramekins** is the same unsweetened baking cocoa as in the ice cream: Dutch-process, which just means the powder has been treated with an alkali so it is darker and less sharp. Plain **kakao** (DecoMorreno, Wawel, Delecta) is already that. **Kakao rozpuszczalne** is sweetened drinking chocolate and will stick and taste wrong.
- **Make ahead:** filled ramekins keep, covered, in the fridge for 24 hours. Freeze them unbaked for up to a month and bake from frozen for **12–14 minutes** at the same temperature. Do not thaw them first.
- **Ramekins:** larger than 150ml, and the same batter makes 3. Smaller dariole moulds (about 120ml) bake in **7–8 minutes**.
- **Egg whites:** two whites are left over. Save them for meringues or freeze for later.
