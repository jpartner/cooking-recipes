# Strawberry & White Chocolate Mascarpone Mousse

No-bake mascarpone and white chocolate mousse layered with juicy macerated strawberries. Makes 4–6 pots and can be made ahead.

## Ingredients

### Mousse

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mascarpone | 250g |
| Double cream | 200ml |
| White chocolate | 100g |
| Icing sugar | 1–2 tbsp (optional) |
| Vanilla extract | 1/2 tsp (optional) |

### Strawberries

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh strawberries, hulled and chopped | 300g |
| Caster sugar | 1 tbsp |

## Method

### 1. Macerate the strawberries

Toss the chopped strawberries with the sugar and leave for 10 minutes until juicy.

### 2. Melt the chocolate

Melt the white chocolate gently — in short bursts in the microwave or in a bowl over barely simmering water. Let it cool slightly so it's still liquid but not hot.

### 3. Make the mousse

Whisk the mascarpone with the melted chocolate (and vanilla, if using) until smooth. Taste before adding icing sugar — white chocolate is already sweet.

Whip the cream to **soft peaks** and fold it gently into the mascarpone mixture.

### 4. Layer and chill

Layer the mousse and strawberries in glasses. Chill for **at least 1 hour**. Finish with grated white chocolate.

## Variations

- **Crispy wonton cups:** brush wonton wrappers with melted butter, sprinkle with sugar (and cinnamon), press into a muffin tin and bake at 180°C for 6–8 minutes. Fill with the mousse and strawberries.
- **Dessert spring rolls:** wrap a spoon of mascarpone, sliced strawberries, and white chocolate chunks in a spring roll wrapper, fry 1–2 minutes until golden, and dust with icing sugar.
- **No-bake cheesecake:** spoon the mousse onto a crushed biscuit and melted butter base and set in the fridge for 3–4 hours.

## Notes

- **Don't overheat the white chocolate.** It seizes easily — low and slow.
- **Cool chocolate, cold mascarpone:** if the chocolate is too hot it will make the mixture grainy; if the mascarpone is too cold the chocolate can set in flecks. Slightly warm chocolate into room-temperature mascarpone is ideal.
- **Sauce:** mash a few strawberries with a little sugar and lemon for a quick drizzle.
- **Keeps:** 2 days refrigerated. Add the strawberries close to serving if making further ahead.
