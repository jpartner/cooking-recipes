# Mushroom Pâté

Simple, savoury mushroom pâté — onion, garlic, parsley, and a splash of cream. Makes about 300g, enough for a starter for 4–6.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mushrooms, sliced or roughly chopped | 400–500g |
| Onion, finely diced | 1 |
| Garlic, chopped | 2–3 cloves |
| Double cream | 3–4 tbsp (or mascarpone for a richer, firmer pâté) |
| Fresh parsley | small handful |
| Butter or oil | 2 tbsp |
| Salt | to taste |
| Black pepper | generous |

## Method

### 1. Soften the onion

Cook the onion in the butter or oil over medium heat for 5–8 minutes until soft, sweet, and golden.

### 2. Cook the mushrooms down

Add the mushrooms with a pinch of salt. Turn the heat up and cook for **10–15 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until **all the liquid has evaporated** and they start to brown. This is the most important step — wet mushrooms give a runny, bland pâté.

### 3. Add the garlic

Stir in the garlic for the last minute of cooking.

### 4. Blend

Let the mixture cool slightly, then blend with the cream and parsley. Pulse for a rustic texture, or blend longer for smooth.

### 5. Season and chill

Season well — plenty of black pepper, salt to taste. Chill for **at least 1 hour** to firm up; the flavour improves as it sits.

## Serving

- On toast or crackers
- With crispy baked wonton wrapper triangles
- With pickles or cornichons to cut the richness

## Notes

- **Depth:** a splash of soy sauce or Worcestershire sauce adds savoury depth.
- **Herbs:** a little thyme cooked with the mushrooms works well.
- **Brightness:** a squeeze of lemon at the end lifts it.
- **Booze:** a dash of brandy or sherry while the mushrooms cook is a classic addition.
- **Keeps:** 3–4 days refrigerated. Pour a thin layer of melted butter over the top to seal it if keeping longer.
