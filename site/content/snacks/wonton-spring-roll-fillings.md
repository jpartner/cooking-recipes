# Wonton & Spring Roll Fillings

Four fillings for shop-bought wonton and spring roll wrappers. Each makes roughly 25–30 wontons or 10–12 spring rolls.

## 1. Pork & Prawn Wontons

The classic wonton filling — juicy, springy, and savoury. Boil, steam, pan-fry, or drop into soup.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pork mince (ideally ~20% fat) | 250g |
| Raw prawns, peeled and deveined | 150–200g |
| Water chestnuts, finely chopped | 3–4 |
| Spring onions, finely sliced | 2 |
| Light soy sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Oyster sauce | 1 tsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| Shaoxing wine or dry sherry | 1 tsp (optional) |
| Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| Sugar | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| White pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Cornflour | 1 tsp |
| Cold water or chicken stock | 2–3 tbsp |

### Method

1. **Prep the prawns:** toss with a pinch of salt and 1/2 tsp cornflour, rinse, and pat very dry. Finely chop half; cut the rest into chunky pieces for bite.
2. **Season the pork:** mix the pork with all the seasonings except the water. Stir hard in one direction for 2–3 minutes until sticky and paste-like.
3. **Add the water** a spoonful at a time, beating it in — this keeps the filling juicy.
4. **Fold in** the prawns, water chestnuts, and spring onion.
5. **Rest** in the fridge for 30–60 minutes before wrapping — this works like a marinade and firms the filling up for easier wrapping.

**Optional extras:** 1 small garlic clove (grated), a few rehydrated dried shiitake (finely chopped), or a little finely grated carrot for colour. This filling also works in spring rolls — add some shredded cabbage and bamboo.

## 2. Beef & Water Chestnut Wontons

Same technique as the pork & prawn, with beef and extra crunch.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beef mince | 250g |
| Water chestnuts, finely chopped | 4–5 |
| Spring onions, finely sliced | 2 |
| Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| Garlic, grated | 1 clove |
| Soy sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| Shaoxing wine or dry sherry | 1 tsp (optional) |
| Sugar | 1/2 tsp |
| White pepper | pinch |
| Cold water or stock | 2–3 tbsp |

### Method

1. Stir the mince with the seasonings hard in one direction for 2–3 minutes until sticky.
2. Beat in the water gradually.
3. Fold in the water chestnuts and spring onion.
4. Use about 1 heaped tsp per wrapper.

## 3. Chicken, Cabbage & Bamboo Spring Rolls

Light and crunchy. Deep-fry or air-fry.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken, finely diced or minced | 250g |
| Cabbage, finely shredded | 1/4 head |
| Carrot, julienned or grated | 1 |
| Bamboo shoots (jar), drained and cut into thin strips | 1/2 jar |
| Spring onions, sliced | 2 |
| Garlic, chopped | 2 cloves |
| Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| Soy sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Oyster sauce (or hoisin) | 1 tbsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| White pepper | pinch |
| Cornflour + water | 1 tsp + 1 tbsp |

### Method

1. Stir-fry the chicken until cooked, then add the garlic and ginger.
2. Add the vegetables and stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes — keep some crunch.
3. Season, then stir in the cornflour slurry to bind the juices.
4. **Cool completely and drain before rolling.** A wet filling makes soggy, split rolls.
5. Roll tightly and seal the edges with a little flour-and-water paste.

## 4. Spiced Beef, Onion & Red Pepper Triangles

A samosa-style twist. Use spring roll wrappers cut into strips and folded into triangles, or wonton wrappers folded in half. Fry or bake.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beef mince | 250g |
| Onion, finely diced | 1 |
| Red pepper, finely diced | 1 |
| Carrot, finely diced | 1 |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| Ground cumin | 1.5 tsp |
| Ground coriander | 1 tsp |
| Garam masala or curry powder | 1 tsp |
| Turmeric | 1/2 tsp |
| Chilli flakes or cayenne | to taste |
| Salt | to taste |
| Lemon juice | squeeze |
| Fresh coriander | handful (optional) |

### Method

1. Soften the onion, then add the garlic, ginger, and spices for 30 seconds.
2. Add the beef and brown it, breaking it up well.
3. Add the pepper and carrot and cook until soft and fairly dry.
4. Season with salt and lemon. Cool before wrapping.

## Dipping Sauces

- **Wontons:** soy sauce, black or rice vinegar, chilli oil, and a pinch of sugar.
- **Spring rolls:** sweet chilli sauce.
- **Spiced triangles:** yoghurt with mint or lime, or mango chutney.

## Notes

- **Keep wrappers covered** with a damp tea towel while you work — they dry out and crack quickly.
- **Test the seasoning:** fry or boil one first before wrapping the whole batch.
- **Don't overfill:** about 1 heaped tsp per wonton, 2 tbsp per spring roll.
- **Freezing:** freeze uncooked wontons and rolls on a tray, then bag them. Cook from frozen — add a couple of minutes.
