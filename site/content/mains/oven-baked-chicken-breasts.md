# Oven-Baked Chicken Breasts

Juicy oven-baked chicken breasts — hot and fast, with an even thickness and a thermometer so they don't dry out. Works for any number of breasts.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breasts | as many as needed |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp per 2 breasts |
| Salt | ~1/2 tsp per breast |
| Black pepper | to taste |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 tsp per 2 breasts |
| Paprika (smoked if you have it) | 1/2 tsp per 2 breasts |
| Sugar | pinch (helps colour) |

### Optional brine

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water | 1 litre |
| Salt | 3 tbsp |

## Method

### 1. Even them out

If one end is much thicker, pound to an even **~2cm** between cling film with a rolling pin — or slice very thick breasts in half horizontally. This stops the thin end drying out before the thick end is cooked.

### 2. Season ahead (if you can)

Salt the breasts **30 minutes to overnight** before cooking, uncovered in the fridge. For extra juiciness, brine instead for **30–60 minutes**, then pat completely dry.

### 3. Oil and season

Rub with oil, then season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and a pinch of sugar.

### 4. Bake

Preheat the oven to **220°C (200°C fan)**. Bake on a lined tray with space between each breast:

- **Regular breasts:** 18–22 minutes
- **Thin or butterflied:** 12–15 minutes

### 5. Check the temperature

Pull them out at **70–72°C** in the thickest part — they'll carry over to 74°C while resting. No thermometer? Cut into the thickest part: it should be white all the way through with clear juices.

### 6. Rest

Rest for **5 minutes** before slicing so the juices stay in the meat.

## Flavour Variations

- **Garlic & herb:** garlic, parsley, lemon zest, and a little butter.
- **Asian-style:** soy, honey, garlic, and ginger; sprinkle with sesame seeds after baking.
- **Spiced:** cumin, coriander, paprika, and chilli.
- **Creamy:** for the last 10 minutes, add a sauce of mushrooms, garlic, and cream to the dish.

## Notes

- **Overcooking is the most common mistake.** A cheap instant-read thermometer is the single best way to guarantee moist chicken.
- **Room temperature:** taking the chicken out of the fridge 20 minutes before baking helps it cook more evenly.
- **Honey or sugar glazes** can burn at 220°C — brush them on in the last 5 minutes.
- **Keeps:** 3–4 days refrigerated. Good sliced cold in salads and sandwiches.
